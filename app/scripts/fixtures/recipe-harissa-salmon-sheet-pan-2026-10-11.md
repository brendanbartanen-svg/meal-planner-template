## Ingredients
<!-- [C] Costco, [K] Kroger, [P] pantry -->
- 4 salmon fillets, ~6 oz each (Costco fresh Atlantic side, portioned) [C]
- 2 tbsp harissa paste [K]
- 1 tbsp olive oil + more for veg [P]
- 1 tsp honey [P]
- 2 medium sweet potatoes, ¾-inch cubes [C]
- 1 large head broccoli, florets [C]
- 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp cumin [P]
- 1 lemon [P]
- Optional drizzle: ½ cup Greek yogurt [P] thinned with lemon juice, pinch of salt

## Steps
1. Oven 425°F. Toss sweet potatoes with oil, smoked paprika, cumin, salt; roast on a sheet pan 15 min.
2. Stir harissa, olive oil, honey; brush over salmon.
3. Push potatoes aside; add broccoli (oiled, salted) and salmon to the pan. Roast 12–14 min until salmon hits 125–130°F.
4. Squeeze lemon over everything; serve with yogurt drizzle.

## Notes
<!-- Cook-to-cook learnings -->
- Reheating note for lunches: salmon reheats best gently (50% microwave power) or eaten room-temp over the veg.
