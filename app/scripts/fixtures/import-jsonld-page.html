Lemony Chickpea Stew with Harissa & Greens

This stew starts with a deeply browned onion and ends with a squeeze of lemon. It is a weeknight dinner that tastes like it simmered all afternoon.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 (15-ounce) cans chickpeas, drained

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high.
  2. Stir in the harissa and chickpeas.
