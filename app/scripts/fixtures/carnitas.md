## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- ~4 lb bone-in pork shoulder, 2-inch chunks [1]
- 1 tbsp neutral oil [P]
- 1 white onion, quartered [2]
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed [P]
- 2 tsp cumin + 1 tsp dried oregano [P]
- 2 bay leaves [P]
- 1 orange, halved [2]
- 1 cup stock [P]
- 12 corn tortillas [2]
- 1 can black beans, warmed [P]
- Cilantro, lime wedges, and diced onion to serve [2]

## Steps
1. Season the pork generously; sear in batches until deeply browned; remove.
2. Add onion and garlic, 2 min. Return pork with cumin, oregano, bay leaves, the squeezed orange halves, and stock.
3. Lid on, 300°F oven or bare simmer, 2.5 hrs until fork-tender.
4. Pull the pork apart; spread on a sheet pan with a few spoonfuls of the braising liquid and broil 5–8 min for crisp edges.
5. Serve in warm tortillas with beans, cilantro, lime, and onion.
