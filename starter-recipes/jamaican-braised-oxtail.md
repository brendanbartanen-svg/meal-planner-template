## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->

**Oxtail**
- 4 lb oxtail, in 1½–2-inch pieces, heavy outer fat trimmed [1 — meat case; the supermarket meat counter is the fallback]
- **Rub:** 2½ tsp kosher salt [P] · 1 tsp black pepper [P] · 1 tbsp ground allspice [2 — small jar, joins the pantry] · ½ tsp cayenne [P] · 1 tbsp soy sauce [P] · 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce [P] · 1 tbsp honey (stands in for the traditional browning sugar) [P]
- 2 tbsp neutral oil [P]
- 1 yellow onion, diced [P]
- 4 scallions, chopped, plus 1 more sliced to finish [2]
- 6 cloves garlic, smashed [P]
- 2 tbsp grated ginger [2 — on this week's list]
- 2 tbsp tomato paste [P]
- 8 sprigs fresh thyme [2]
- 2 bay leaves [P]
- 1 whole scotch bonnet (or habanero) [2]
- 2 carrots, in thick coins [2]
- 3 cups beef or chicken stock (or better-than-bouillon) [P]
- 1 can (15 oz) butter beans, drained and rinsed [2]
- Juice of ½ lime to finish [P — or 2 tsp red wine vinegar]

**Rice & peas**
- 1 cup long-grain rice (jasmine or basmati), rinsed [P]
- 1 can (15 oz) kidney beans, drained and rinsed [2]
- ¾ cup coconut milk [P] · ¾ cup water
- 2 scallions, bruised with the flat of a knife [2] · 3 sprigs thyme [2] · 1 clove garlic, smashed [P] · ½ tsp ground allspice [2] · ¾ tsp kosher salt [P]

**Garlicky steamed cabbage**
- ½ medium green cabbage, cored and shredded [2 — ~1½ lb]
- 1 carrot, cut in matchsticks [2]
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced [P]
- 1 tbsp butter [P] · 1 tbsp olive oil [P] · 2 sprigs thyme [2] · salt, black pepper

## Prep
- **Oxtail (as soon as it's home — 1 h floor, overnight is better):** trim any heavy outer fat, pat dry, toss with the whole rub, and refrigerate. The salt has to get into the meat itself — the gravy can't season it from outside.
- Oven to 325°F.
- **Braise:** dice the onion; chop the scallions, holding 1 back to slice for the finish; smash the garlic; grate the ginger; cut the carrots into thick coins; drain and rinse the butter beans; halve the lime.
- **Rice & peas (any time during the braise):** rinse the rice; drain and rinse the kidney beans; bruise the scallions with the flat of a knife; smash the garlic.
- **Cabbage (any time during the braise):** core and shred the cabbage; cut the carrot into matchsticks; slice the garlic.

## Steps
1. **Brown (~2 pm):** heat the oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high and brown the oxtail hard on all sides, in two batches, 4–5 min a side. The honey in the rub darkens fast — mahogany is the goal, black is not. Remove; pour off all but ~1 tbsp fat.
2. **Build:** onion and chopped scallions into the pot, 4–5 min, scraping up the fond. Garlic, ginger and tomato paste, 1–2 min until the paste darkens. Oxtail back in with the thyme, bay, stock and the **whole, uncut** scotch bonnet. The liquid should come about three-quarters up the meat. Bring to a simmer, lid on, into the oven.
3. **Braise 3 h**, turning the pieces once at the halfway mark and adding the carrots then. Done when the meat pulls from the bone with a fork but still holds on.
4. **Finish the gravy:** oxtail out to a plate. Skim the fat hard — there will be a lot, and skimming it is the difference between rich and greasy. Fish out the thyme stems and bay. For medium-hot, pierce the scotch bonnet with a knife tip and leave it in; for hot, mash half of it into the gravy; then discard the rest. Add the butter beans and simmer uncovered on the stove 10–15 min until the gravy coats a spoon. Oxtail back in, lime juice, taste for salt. It should read deeply savory, allspice-warm, with real heat at the back.
5. **Rice & peas (start ~35 min before dinner):** everything into a saucepan, bring to a boil, lid on, lowest heat 15 min, then 10 min off heat untouched. Pull the scallions, thyme and garlic; fluff.
6. **Cabbage (last 10 min):** butter and olive oil in a wide skillet over medium-high, garlic 30 seconds, then cabbage, carrot, thyme, ¾ tsp salt, plenty of black pepper and 2 tbsp water. Lid on 4–5 min, lid off 2 min more — tender-crisp, not soft. Season it in its own right.
7. Plate rice & peas, oxtail and butter beans with plenty of gravy over, cabbage alongside, sliced scallion on top.

**Instant Pot fallback (shop ran late):** brown on sauté in batches, build the base in the pot, cut the stock to 2 cups, 45 min high pressure + 15 min natural release. Skim, then reduce the gravy with the butter beans on sauté 10 min.
