## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 4 cod fillets, ~6 oz each, fresh [1]
- 4 tbsp olive oil, plus more to finish [P]
- 1 shallot or ½ yellow onion, thinly sliced [P]
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced [P]
- ¾ tsp red pepper flakes [P]
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved [1]
- ½ cup kalamata or castelvetrano olives, smashed and pitted [P]
- 3 tbsp capers, drained [2]
- 1 cup dry white wine [2]
- 1 cup water
- Big handful parsley, chopped [2]
- 1 lemon [P]
- Crusty bread, grilled or toasted [2]
- Salt

## Prep
- Thinly slice the shallot and garlic; pepper flakes measured.
- Halve the tomatoes. Smash and pit the olives; drain the capers.
- Measure the wine and water.
- Salt the cod.
- Chop the parsley, cut the lemon, slice the bread.

## Steps
1. Sweat the shallot, garlic, and pepper flakes in the olive oil over medium until fragrant. Add the tomatoes with a good pinch of salt and cook until they burst and start to jam, 5–6 min.
2. Add the wine and reduce by half. Add the water, olives, and capers; simmer 5 min into a loose, briny broth. Taste and season assertively — the fish will dilute it, and this broth is the whole dish.
3. Nestle the salted cod into the broth, lid on, and poach at a bare simmer 6–8 min until it just flakes (~130°F). Grill or toast the bread meanwhile.
4. Off heat: parsley, a squeeze of lemon, and a real drizzle of olive oil over the top. Serve in shallow bowls with the bread for mopping.
