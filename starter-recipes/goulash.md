## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- ~2.5–3 lb chuck roast, 1.5-inch cubes [1]
- 2 tbsp neutral oil [P]
- 2 yellow onions, diced [P]
- 4 cloves garlic, minced [P]
- 3 tbsp sweet paprika + 1 tsp smoked paprika [P]
- 1 tsp caraway seeds (optional — skip if not on hand) [2]
- 2 tbsp tomato paste [P]
- 2 bell peppers, chopped [1]
- 2 cups stock [P]
- 1 lb Yukon gold potatoes, chunks [2]
- Sour cream or Greek yogurt to serve [P]

## Prep
- **Beef:** trim the chuck and cut it into 1.5-inch cubes; pat dry and season well with salt and pepper.
- Oven to 300°F if braising there.
- Dice the onions; mince the garlic.
- Paprikas and caraway into one small bowl — they go in off the heat, fast. Tomato paste and stock measured alongside.
- **Last-hour veg (now, or during the braise):** chop the peppers; cut the potatoes into chunks and hold them in cold water.

## Steps
1. Sear the beef hard in the oil in batches; remove.
2. Soften the onions 6–8 min. Garlic, tomato paste, 1 min. Off heat, stir in the paprika (it burns easily) + caraway.
3. Return the beef with the stock; scrape the fond. Lid on, 300°F oven or bare simmer, 2 hrs.
4. Add the peppers and potatoes; 45–60 min more until the beef is spoon-tender.
5. Season, serve with a dollop of yogurt/sour cream.

## Notes
<!-- Cook-to-cook learnings -->
- Sunday food: 3 hours total means it needs a weekend, and the ~48 h thaw on a 3 lb roast means the chuck has to leave the freezer by Friday.
