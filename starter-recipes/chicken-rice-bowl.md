## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs [1]
- Marinade: 3 tbsp soy sauce [P], 1 tbsp honey [P], 1 tbsp rice vinegar [P], 2 cloves garlic [P], 1 tsp grated ginger [P], 1 tbsp neutral oil [P]
- 2 cups jasmine rice [P]
- 1 large head broccoli, cut into florets [1]
- 2 Persian cucumbers, thinly sliced [2]
- 2 carrots, shredded [2]
- Quick pickle: 3 tbsp rice vinegar [P], 1 tsp honey [P], pinch of salt [P]
- Drizzle: ½ cup Greek yogurt [P] + 1–2 tsp hot sauce [P] + squeeze of lime [P] (or mayo-sriracha)
- Scallions and sesame seeds to finish [2]

## Prep
- **Chicken:** grate the garlic and ginger, whisk the marinade, and coat the thighs — 15+ min, or morning-of.
- Rice on. Oven to 425°F (or air fryer to 400°F).
- **Broccoli:** cut into florets; toss with oil, salt, and pepper.
- **Pickled veg:** thinly slice the cucumbers, shred the carrots, toss with the quick-pickle mix and set aside.
- **Drizzle:** stir the yogurt, hot sauce, and lime together.
- Slice the scallions. Blackstone on (or a skillet heating) for the chicken.

## Steps
1. Roast the broccoli at 425°F ~18 min (or air fryer 400°F ~10 min).
2. Griddle the chicken on the Blackstone (or hard sear in a skillet) ~4 min/side to 165°F, rest, then slice.
3. Build bowls: rice, chicken, broccoli, pickled veg, drizzle, scallions/sesame.
