## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 1½ lb fresh firm white fish (cod, mahi, halibut — whatever looks best) [1/2 — the warehouse club fresh counter if stocked, else the supermarket seafood counter; never frozen]
- **Rub:** 1 tbsp chili powder [P] · 2 tsp smoked paprika [P] · 1 tsp cumin [P] · 1 tsp garlic powder [P] · ½ tsp cayenne [P] · 1¼ tsp kosher salt
- Neutral oil for the griddle [P]
- 12 corn tortillas [2 — 3 per serving; the warehouse club's count is restaurant-sized]
- **Chipotle-lime crema:** ¾ cup Greek yogurt [P] · ¼ cup mayonnaise [P] · 2 chipotles in adobo, minced, + 1 tbsp of the adobo sauce [2] · zest + juice of 1 lime [P] · ½ tsp salt
- **Charred cabbage slaw:** ½ small green cabbage, sliced into thin ribbons [2] · 1 tbsp neutral oil [P] · juice of 1 lime [P] · ½ tsp salt · ½ cup chopped cilantro [2]
- 2 avocados, sliced [2]
- Lime wedges to serve [P]

## Prep
- **Fish:** mix the rub spices. Salt the fish directly on both sides (a good tsp of the total salt; the rest goes in the rub), then coat with the rub. Room temp while the griddle heats — the rub needs 15–20 min to grip.
- Blackstone on **high** to heat.
- **Chipotle-lime crema:** mince the chipotles; stir them into the yogurt and mayo with the adobo sauce, the lime zest and juice, and the salt. Refrigerate. It should be assertively smoky-hot on its own — it's the only sauce on the taco.
- **Slaw:** slice the cabbage into thin ribbons, chop the cilantro, juice the slaw lime.
- Last, just before heat: slice the avocados, cut the lime wedges, count out the tortillas.

## Steps
1. One zone of the griddle lightly oiled. Pile the cabbage on the dry-ish hot zone and leave it alone 2–3 min until the edges char black in spots, flip the pile once, then pull to a bowl and toss with the lime, salt, and cilantro while hot. Charred-but-crunchy, not steamed.
2. Fish on the oiled zone: 2–3 min a side untouched until deeply browned, then break it into big flakes with the spatula edge and give the flakes 30 seconds cut-side down. Kill the heat.
3. Toast the tortillas on the griddle ~20 sec a side, doubling them up per taco.
4. Build: tortilla, crema swiped first, fish, charred slaw, avocado, more cilantro, lime squeeze at the table.
