## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs [1 — 6-lb pack; see prep_ahead for the freezer split]
- **Glaze:** 3 tbsp gochujang [2 — tub keeps ~forever refrigerated, joins the pantry] · 2 tbsp honey [P] · 2 tbsp soy sauce [P] · 1 tbsp rice vinegar [P] · 4 cloves garlic, grated [P] · 1 tbsp grated ginger [P] · 1 tsp toasted sesame oil [P] · 2 tbsp water
- **Smashed cucumbers:** 2 cucumbers [2] · 1 tsp kosher salt [P] · 1 tbsp rice vinegar [P] · 2 tsp toasted sesame oil [P] · 1 tsp sesame seeds [P] · ½ tsp red pepper flakes (or gochugaru) [P] · ½ tsp honey [P]
- 1½ cups jasmine rice [P]
- 3 scallions, sliced [2] · more sesame seeds to finish [P]
- 1 tbsp neutral oil for the sear [P]

## Prep
- Rice on.
- **Smashed cucumbers:** whack with the flat of a knife or a pan, rip into craggy chunks, toss with the salt, 15 min in a colander.
- **Glaze:** grate the garlic and ginger, whisk everything together.
- **Chicken:** salt the thighs directly on both sides — season the component, not just the sauce around it.
- Slice the scallions.
- **Cucumbers, once they've had their 15 min:** dress with the vinegar, sesame oil, seeds, pepper flakes, honey — cold and punchy against the glaze.

## Steps
1. Sear the thighs in a hot skillet with the neutral oil, 5–6 min a side to deep browning and cooked through — two batches, don't crowd.
2. Pour off excess fat, everything back in, glaze in. Toss over medium 2–3 min until it reduces to a sticky lacquer that coats. It should read glossy and medium-hot — the gochujang and honey do the balancing; no table correction needed.
3. Thighs over rice, pan glaze spooned over, scallions + sesame seeds. Cucumbers on the side.
