## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, pounded to an even ½ inch [1]
- Salt, black pepper [P]
- ⅔ cup flour, for dredging [P]
- 2 tbsp olive oil, for the sear [P]
- **Pan sauce:** 3 cloves garlic, minced [P] · ½ cup dry white wine [2] · 1 cup chicken stock [P] · juice + zest of 1½ lemons [P] · 3–4 tbsp capers, drained [P] · 2 tbsp cold butter, to mount [P]
- Big handful chopped parsley [2]
- 1.5–2 lb broccoli crowns, cut into florets [1]
- Olive oil + red pepper flakes for the broccoli [P]

## Prep
- Oven to 425°F. Broccoli into florets, tossed on a sheet pan with olive oil, salt, and red pepper flakes.
- **Chicken:** pound the thighs to an even ½ inch. Season aggressively with salt and pepper — they're the whole dish, don't be shy. Flour into a shallow dish.
- **Pan sauce:** mince the garlic; zest and juice the lemons; drain the capers; measure the wine and stock. The butter stays in the fridge until it's needed cold.
- Chop the parsley.
- Last, right before the pan: dredge the thighs in flour and shake off the excess.

## Steps
1. Broccoli into the oven; roast 18–20 min until charred at the edges while the chicken goes.
2. Sear the thighs in the olive oil over medium-high, 4–5 min per side to deep golden and cooked through. Two batches; crowding steams them. Rest on a plate.
3. Same pan: garlic 30 sec, then wine to deglaze — scrape hard — and reduce by half. Add stock and reduce until it coats a spoon, 3–4 min.
4. Off heat: lemon juice + zest, capers, and the cold butter swirled in until glossy. Taste — it should be sharp and briny enough to make you blink; if it reads flat, more lemon and salt, not less.
5. Return the thighs and their juices to the pan for a minute to nap in the sauce. Parsley over everything; serve with the broccoli for dragging through the sauce.
