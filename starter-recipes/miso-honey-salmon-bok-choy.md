## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 4 skin-on salmon fillets, ~6 oz each (the warehouse club fresh Atlantic side, portioned — fresh counter only) [1]
- Kosher salt for the dry brine [P]
- **Glaze:** 3 tbsp white (shiro) miso [2 — small tub, keeps months refrigerated, joins the pantry] · 2 tbsp honey [P] · 1 tbsp soy sauce [P] · 1 tbsp rice vinegar [P] · 1 tsp toasted sesame oil [P] · 1 tbsp grated ginger [P] · 1 clove garlic, grated [P]
- **Chili-scallion oil:** 5 scallions, thinly sliced — whites/light greens for the oil, dark greens reserved to finish [2] · 3 cloves garlic, minced [P] · 1 tbsp red pepper flakes (or gochugaru) [P] · 1 tsp sesame seeds [P] · ¼ cup neutral oil [P] · 1 tbsp soy sauce [P] · 1 tsp rice vinegar [P] · ½ tsp honey [P]
- 1½ lb baby bok choy, halved lengthwise, rinsed and dried well [2 — 6–8 heads]
- 1 tbsp neutral oil for the sear [P]
- 1 cup jasmine rice [P]
- Lime or lemon wedges [P]

## Prep
- **Salmon:** salt directly on both sides and set uncovered in the fridge 30–45 min — the fish needs to taste seasoned on its own, not just under the glaze.
- Rice on.
- **Chili-scallion oil:** thinly slice the scallions, whites and light greens apart from the dark greens (reserved to finish); mince the garlic. Scallion whites, garlic, pepper flakes, sesame seeds and a pinch of salt into a heatproof bowl; the soy, vinegar and honey measured together alongside.
- **Glaze:** grate the ginger and garlic; whisk with the miso, honey, soy, vinegar and sesame oil.
- **Bok choy:** halve lengthwise, rinse, and dry well.
- Line a sheet pan with foil and oil it lightly; broiler on high, rack 5–6 in from the element. Cut the lime or lemon wedges.

## Steps
1. Chili-scallion oil: heat its neutral oil until a scallion slice sizzles hard on contact (~325°F), pour it over the bowl — it should bloom and crackle. Stir in the soy, vinegar and honey. It should read hot and punchy; add flakes if it doesn't.
2. Pat the salmon dry, skin-side down on the prepared sheet pan; brush with half the glaze.
3. Broil 6–8 min, brushing with the rest of the glaze at the halfway mark, until lacquered with charred spots and 125°F at the center. Miso + honey goes from mahogany to burnt fast — watch it, and tent any runaway spots with foil.
4. Meanwhile, bok choy: skillet (or the Blackstone) screaming hot with the sear oil, cut-side down, don't touch for 2–3 min until deeply charred. Flip, pinch of salt, splash of water, lid on 1 min to steam the stems tender-crisp.
5. Plate rice, bok choy and salmon; spoon the chili-scallion oil over the fish and greens, scatter the reserved scallion greens, lime on the side.
