## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 4 skinless cod or halibut fillets, ~6 oz each, thick center cuts (fresh counter only) [1]
- Kosher salt for the dry brine [P]
- **Chili salsa verde:** 1 large bunch flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped [2 — ~1½ cups packed leaves] · 2 tbsp capers, rinsed and chopped [2 — small jar; skip the buy if the August jar still has them] · 3 anchovy fillets, minced [2 — small tin] · 1 clove garlic, grated [P] · 2 tsp chopped Calabrian chili in oil [2 — small jar; 1 tsp pantry red pepper flakes is the fallback if the supermarket doesn't stock it] · zest of 1 lemon + 2 tbsp juice [P] · 1 tsp Dijon mustard [P] · 1 tsp red wine vinegar [P] · ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil [P]
- **Smashed potatoes:** 1½ lb baby gold potatoes [2] · 2 tbsp olive oil [P] · salt, black pepper
- **Charred broccoli:** 1¼ lb broccoli crowns, cut into long florets [2] · 1 tbsp olive oil [P] · pinch of red pepper flakes [P] · salt
- **For the fish:** 1 tbsp neutral oil [P] · 1 tbsp butter [P] · 1 clove garlic, smashed [P]
- Lemon wedges [P]

## Prep
- Oven to 450°F with two sheet pans inside to preheat.
- **Potatoes:** into cold, well-salted water and bring to a simmer — they cook through the rest of the prep.
- **Fish:** salt directly on both sides and set uncovered in the fridge 30–45 min.
- **Salsa verde:** finely chop the parsley; rinse and chop the capers; mince the anchovies; grate the garlic; zest and juice the lemon. Stir everything together and season with salt. It should taste too sharp, too salty and distinctly hot on its own — it is seasoning for mild fish and plain potatoes. Add chili until it has a real kick.
- **Broccoli:** cut the crowns into long florets.
- **For the fish:** smash the garlic; cut the lemon wedges.

## Steps
1. **Potatoes:** simmer until a knife slides in, 12–15 min. Drain and steam dry 2 min. Onto one hot sheet pan, smash each to ~½ inch with a glass, drizzle with the olive oil, salt and pepper. Roast 25–30 min, flipping once, until deep golden and crisp at the edges.
2. **Broccoli:** toss with the oil, salt and pepper flakes; onto the second hot sheet pan for the potatoes' last 15 min, undisturbed, until charred at the tips and tender-crisp at the stem.
3. **Fish:** pat very dry. Neutral oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high until shimmering; fish in presentation-side down and leave it alone 3–4 min for a real golden crust. Flip, add the butter and smashed garlic, baste 30 seconds, then move the skillet to the oven 2–4 min — cod to 135°F, halibut to 130°F. Cod flakes apart if handled twice, so flip once with a fish spatula.
4. Plate potatoes and broccoli, fish on top, salsa verde spooned generously over all three, lemon wedge on the side.
