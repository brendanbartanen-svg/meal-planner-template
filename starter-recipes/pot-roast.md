## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 3 lb chuck roast [1]
- 2 tbsp neutral oil [P]
- 2 yellow onions, wedged [P]
- 5 carrots, big chunks [1]
- 1.5 lb Yukon gold potatoes, halved [1]
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed [P]
- 2 tbsp tomato paste [P]
- 2 cups chicken/beef stock [P]
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire [P]
- 1 tsp dried thyme, 2 bay leaves [P]
- Salt, pepper [P]

## Prep
- Oven to 325°F.
- **Roast:** pat dry and season aggressively with salt and pepper on all sides.
- **Braise:** cut the onions into wedges; smash the garlic; stir the Worcestershire into the stock; thyme and bay leaves out.
- **Vegetables (any time in the first 2 h):** cut the carrots into big chunks; halve the potatoes.

## Steps
1. Sear the roast hard in the Dutch oven with the oil, 4–5 min per side. Remove.
2. Soften the onions 5 min; add the garlic and tomato paste, 1 min. Deglaze with the stock + Worcestershire, scraping the fond.
3. Return the roast with the thyme and bay. Lid on, into the oven 2 hrs.
4. Add the carrots and potatoes; braise 1–1.5 hrs more until the beef shreds and veg is tender.
5. Rest the meat, skim the fat, reduce the braising liquid a few minutes if thin. Season, slice/shred, serve over the veg with the jus.
