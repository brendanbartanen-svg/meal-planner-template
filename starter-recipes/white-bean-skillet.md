## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 3 cans (15 oz) cannellini or great northern beans, drained [2]
- 3 tbsp olive oil [P]
- 1 yellow onion, diced [P]
- 5 cloves garlic, sliced [P]
- 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp oregano, ½ tsp red pepper flakes [P]
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes [P]
- 5 oz baby spinach [1]
- 6 oz feta, in chunks [1]
- 4 pitas, warmed [2]
- Squeeze of lemon [P]

## Prep
- Dice the onion; slice the garlic.
- Measure the smoked paprika, oregano and pepper flakes into one small bowl.
- Drain the beans; open the tomatoes.
- Cut the feta into chunks; cut the lemon; pitas ready to warm.

## Steps
1. Soften the onion in olive oil, 5–6 min. Garlic + spices, 1 min.
2. Add the tomatoes; simmer 8–10 min until thick. Season.
3. Fold in the beans + a splash of water; simmer 5 min. Wilt in the spinach.
4. Nestle the feta chunks on top, lid on 2 min to soften. Lemon squeeze, olive-oil drizzle, warm pitas for scooping.

## Notes
<!-- Cook-to-cook learnings -->
- Broiler-safe skillet: 2 min under the broiler browns the feta nicely.
