## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 4 salmon fillets, ~6 oz each [1 — the warehouse club fresh Atlantic side, portioned]
- 2 tbsp harissa paste [2]
- 1 tbsp olive oil + more for veg [P]
- 1 tsp honey [P]
- 2 medium sweet potatoes, ¾-inch cubes [1]
- 1 large head broccoli, florets [1]
- 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp cumin [P]
- 1 lemon [P]
- Optional drizzle: ½ cup Greek yogurt [P] thinned with lemon juice, pinch of salt

## Prep
- Oven to 425°F.
- **Sweet potatoes:** cut into ¾-inch cubes; toss on a sheet pan with oil, the smoked paprika, cumin, and salt.
- **Broccoli:** cut into florets; oil and salt them.
- **Salmon:** portion the side if it came whole, pat dry. Stir the harissa, olive oil, and honey together and brush it over the fillets.
- **Drizzle:** thin the yogurt with lemon juice and a pinch of salt.

## Steps
1. Sweet potatoes into the oven; roast 15 min.
2. Push the potatoes aside; add the broccoli and salmon to the pan. Roast 12–14 min until the salmon hits 125–130°F.
3. Squeeze lemon over everything; serve with the yogurt drizzle.

## Notes
<!-- Cook-to-cook learnings -->
- Reheating note for lunches: salmon reheats best gently (50% microwave power) or eaten room-temp over the veg.
