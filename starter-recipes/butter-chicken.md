## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, bite-size pieces [1]
- Marinade: ¾ cup Greek yogurt [P], 1 tbsp garam masala [P], 1 tsp turmeric [P], 1 tsp cumin [P], 4 cloves garlic [P], 1 tbsp ginger [P], salt
- 2 tbsp ghee or butter [P]
- 1 large yellow onion, diced [P]
- 1 tbsp ginger + 3 cloves garlic, minced [P]
- 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp cayenne (medium) [P]
- 1 can crushed tomatoes [P — 28-oz can; the half left from Tuesday's chana masala]
- ¼ cup coconut milk [P] + ½ cup heavy cream [2]
- 1 tsp honey [P], squeeze of lemon [P]
- Cilantro [2]
- 1½ cups basmati rice [P]

## Prep
- **Chicken (morning-of, or 30+ min ahead):** cut the thighs into bite-size pieces. Grate the marinade garlic and ginger, stir them into the yogurt with the spices and salt, and coat the chicken.
- Rice on.
- Broiler on high; line a sheet pan with foil.
- **Sauce:** dice the onion; mince the ginger and garlic. Ground spices into one small bowl. Open the tomatoes.
- **Finish:** measure the coconut milk, cream, and honey; cut the lemon; chop the cilantro.

## Steps
1. Spread the marinated chicken on the foil-lined sheet and broil ~8–10 min until charred at the edges (air fryer 400°F works too; kamado if it's already lit).
2. Meanwhile: soften the onion in the ghee 6–8 min; add the garlic/ginger 1 min; add the spices 30 sec; add the tomatoes and simmer 10 min.
3. Blend the sauce smooth (immersion blender), stir in the coconut milk, honey, lemon; season. Swirl in the cream at the end, off the boil.
4. Fold in the charred chicken + any juices; simmer 5 min. Serve over basmati with cilantro.
