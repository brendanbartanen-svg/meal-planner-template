## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 1½ lb **fresh** ahi/yellowfin tuna steaks, ~1" thick [1/2 — fresh seafood case only, never frozen; the warehouse club fresh counter if stocked, else the supermarket seafood counter]
- 4 tbsp sesame seeds (white, or white + black) [2]
- 1½ cups jasmine rice [P]
- **Quick-pickled cucumber:** 2 cucumbers, thinly sliced [2] · ⅓ cup rice vinegar [P] · 1 tsp sugar or honey [P] · 1 tsp salt
- **Ginger-scallion sauce:** 1 bunch scallions, minced [2] · 2 tbsp grated ginger [P] · ⅓ cup neutral oil, heated smoking-hot [P] · 1 tbsp soy sauce [P] · ½ tsp salt
- **Sriracha mayo:** 3 tbsp mayonnaise [P] · 1–2 tbsp sriracha or chili-garlic sauce [P] · squeeze of lime [P]
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil [2]
- Soy sauce for the table [P]
- Neutral oil for the sear [P]

## Prep
- Rice on first.
- **Pickles:** thinly slice the cucumbers and toss with the vinegar, sweetener, and salt; refrigerate — they pickle while everything else happens.
- **Ginger-scallion sauce:** mince the scallions, grate the ginger, and put both in a heatproof bowl with the salt. Oil measured into a small pan, soy alongside.
- **Sriracha mayo:** whisk the mayo, sriracha, and lime.
- **Tuna:** pat the steaks very dry, rub with a little neutral oil and the sesame oil, salt well, then press all sides into the sesame seeds for a full crust.

## Steps
1. **Ginger-scallion sauce:** heat the oil until it just smokes and pour it over the scallions and ginger — it should sizzle hard. Stir in the soy.
2. Get a heavy skillet **ripping hot** — the sear is 45–60 seconds per side, sesame toasted and deep golden, center still rare. This is the whole dish: if the pan isn't hot enough you'll cook it through before the crust sets. Rest 2 min, slice against the grain in ½" slabs.
3. Build bowls: rice, fanned tuna, drained pickles, a heavy spoon of ginger-scallion sauce over the fish, sriracha mayo zigzagged over the top.

## Notes
<!-- Cook-to-cook learnings -->
- Rare center is the target; if a steak runs thin (<¾"), drop to ~30 sec a side.
