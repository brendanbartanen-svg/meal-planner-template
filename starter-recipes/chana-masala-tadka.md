## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 2 cans chickpeas, drained, liquid reserved [P — 15-oz cans]
- 4 tbsp ghee — 2 for the gravy, 2 for the tadka [P]
- 1 tsp cumin seeds [P]
- 1 large yellow onion, finely diced [P]
- 6 cloves garlic — 4 minced (gravy), 2 thinly sliced (tadka) [P]
- 1 tbsp grated ginger [P]
- 2 serrano chiles, minced, seeds in — the gravy's heat is built here, not corrected at the table [2]
- 1 tbsp ground coriander · 2 tsp ground cumin · 1 tsp turmeric · 1 tsp smoked paprika · ½ tsp cayenne [P]
- 2 cups crushed tomatoes [P — most of a 28-oz can]
- 1½ tsp garam masala (finish) [P]
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes (tadka) [P]
- 1 lemon [P]
- ½ cup Greek yogurt, for swirling [P]
- Big handful cilantro, chopped [2]
- 1½ cups basmati rice [P]

## Prep
- Start the basmati.
- **Gravy:** finely dice the onion; mince the gravy garlic, grate the ginger, mince the serranos with their seeds.
- **Spices:** ground spices into one small bowl; cumin seeds and the gravy ghee by the stove.
- Measure out the tomatoes. Drain the chickpeas over a bowl, keeping the liquid.
- **Tadka:** thinly slice the tadka garlic; tadka ghee and pepper flakes by a small pan.
- **Finish:** measure the garam masala, chop the cilantro, halve the lemon — one half for juice, the other in wedges.

## Steps
1. Bloom the cumin seeds in the gravy ghee over medium-high, ~30 sec. Add the onion with 1 tsp salt and cook it hard, 9–10 min, to deep brown — the color is the gravy's backbone; don't pull it at golden.
2. Garlic, ginger, serranos: 1 min. Ground spices: 30 sec, toasting in the fat. Add the tomatoes and simmer briskly 6–8 min until thick and the ghee stains the surface.
3. Add the chickpeas plus ~¾ cup of their liquid; simmer 12 min, smashing a handful against the pot to thicken. Salt to bold — taste it with rice, not alone.
4. Kill the heat; stir in the garam masala and the juice of half the lemon.
5. Tadka: in the small pan, heat the tadka ghee over medium-high, fry the sliced garlic to golden, pull it off the heat, add the pepper flakes for 10 sec, and pour it sizzling over the pot.
6. Serve over rice with a swirl of yogurt, the cilantro, and the rest of the lemon in wedges.
