## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 2 blocks (14–16 oz each) extra-firm tofu [2 — the warehouse club doesn't sell single blocks sensibly; keeps for weeks, this is the at-risk night]
- **Tofu marinade:** 1 tbsp soy sauce [P] · ¾ tsp kosher salt [P] · 1½ tsp garam masala [P] · ½ tsp turmeric [P] · ½ tsp cayenne [P] · 1 tbsp neutral oil [P]
- 2 tbsp cornstarch [2 — small box, joins the pantry]
- **Saag:** 2 tbsp ghee or butter [P] · 1 tsp cumin seeds [P] · 1 yellow onion, diced [P] · 6 cloves garlic, minced [P] · 2 tbsp grated ginger [P] · 2 serranos (or jalapeños), minced [2] · 1 lb frozen chopped spinach [1 — bulk bag, remainder stays a freezer staple] · 2 tsp garam masala [P] · ½ tsp turmeric [P] · ½ tsp cayenne [P] · ⅓ cup heavy cream [2/P] · squeeze of lemon [P]
- 1½ cups basmati rice [P]

## Prep
- **Tofu (morning — 4+ h ideal, 1 h floor):** press ~30 min, tear/cut into big craggy cubes, salt them directly, then toss in the marinade. Fridge until dinner — the paneer-tikka lesson applies whole: the component gets seasoned in its own right, not just the gravy around it.
- Rice on.
- **Spinach:** into a covered pan with a splash of water to thaw; drain lightly, keeping some liquid.
- **Saag:** dice the onion; mince the garlic and serranos; grate the ginger; measure the garam masala, turmeric and cayenne into one small bowl, the cumin seeds into another.
- Measure the cornstarch; cut a lemon wedge for the squeeze.

## Steps
1. Saag base: ghee, cumin seeds until they sputter; onion hard 8–10 min to real browning. Garlic, ginger, serranos, dry spices, 1–2 min. Spinach in; simmer 8–10 min. A few pulses with the immersion blender — rough, keep texture. Season assertively; the cream comes next and mutes everything.
2. Char the tofu: toss the marinated cubes with the cornstarch; into a smoking-hot cast iron with a good slick of oil, undisturbed until deeply browned and charred at the edges, turning once or twice — 6–8 min, two batches if needed. The char is the flavor.
3. Cream into the saag, then serve the tofu over the top (or fold gently at the last second — the crust is the point). Lemon squeeze, taste salt again. Over basmati.
