## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 1½ cups red lentils, rinsed [P]
- 1 yellow onion, diced [P]
- 4 cloves garlic, minced [P]
- 1 tbsp grated ginger [P]
- 1 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp ground coriander, 1 tsp cumin [P]
- 1 tsp salt, plus more
- 5 oz baby spinach [1]
- Juice of 1 lemon [P]
- 1½ cups basmati rice [P]
- Naan to serve, optional [2]
- Cilantro [2]

**Tadka (the finish — this is the whole dish, don't skip it):**
- 3 tbsp ghee or butter [P]
- 1 tsp cumin seeds [P]
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes [P]
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced [P]
- ½ tsp smoked paprika [P]

## Prep
- Rice on.
- Rinse the lentils.
- Dice the onion; mince the garlic; grate the ginger; measure the turmeric, coriander and cumin into one small bowl.
- Juice the lemon; chop the cilantro.
- **Tadka:** thinly slice the garlic; cumin seeds in one pinch bowl, pepper flakes and paprika together in another (they go in at the same moment); ghee by the small pan.

## Steps
1. Sweat the onion in a little ghee 6–8 min; add the garlic and ginger, 1 min; add the turmeric, coriander and cumin, 30 sec.
2. Add the lentils, 4 cups water and the measured salt. Simmer 18–22 min, stirring now and then, until the lentils collapse into a loose porridge. Add water if it tightens too far — it should pour, not mound.
3. Stir in the spinach until wilted. Season aggressively with salt and the lemon juice; dal tastes flat until both are right.
4. **Tadka:** heat the ghee in a small pan until shimmering. Add the cumin seeds (they'll pop, ~20 sec), then the sliced garlic until it just turns gold, then the pepper flakes and paprika off the heat — 5 seconds, or the spices scorch.
5. Pour the tadka over the dal at the table so it crackles. Serve over rice with cilantro and naan.

## Notes
<!-- Cook-to-cook learnings -->
- The tadka is where the flavor lives — it's the same "finishing fat" move that made the white bean skillet and rice bowl land at 5. Pour it on at the last second, not during the simmer.
- Salt and acid are the usual fix if it tastes dull. Go further than feels right on both.
