## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 14 oz paneer, in 1" cubes [1 — 2-pack; the second block keeps for weeks]
- **Tikka marinade:** ½ cup Greek yogurt [P] · 1 tbsp lemon juice [P] · 1 tsp garam masala [P] · 1 tsp smoked paprika [P] · ½ tsp turmeric [P] · ½ tsp salt
- **Gravy:** 3 tbsp ghee or butter [P] · 1 large yellow onion, diced [P] · 5 cloves garlic, minced [P] · 1 tbsp grated ginger [P] · 2 tbsp tomato paste [P] · 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes [P] · 2 tsp garam masala [P] · 1 tsp cumin [P] · 1 tsp ground coriander [P] · ½–1 tsp cayenne [P] · 3 crushed cardamom pods [P] · ¾ cup heavy cream [2]
- 1½ cups basmati rice [P]
- Cilantro [2] · lemon wedges [P]

## Prep
- **Paneer:** cut into 1" cubes and toss in the marinade — 30 min on the counter while the gravy builds is enough (up to 2 h in the fridge).
- Rice on.
- **Gravy:** dice the onion; mince the garlic; grate the ginger; measure the garam masala, cumin, coriander and cayenne into one small bowl; crush the cardamom pods.
- Foil a sheet pan for the paneer (or set out the cast-iron); chop the cilantro; cut the lemon wedges.

## Steps
1. Gravy: ghee, then the onion hard for 8–10 min to real browning — the color is the depth. Garlic, ginger, tomato paste, and the dry spices, 1–2 min until the paste darkens.
2. Crushed tomatoes + cardamom; simmer 10–12 min until thick and the fat breaks to the edges. Season assertively — the cream comes next and mutes everything, so the gravy should taste slightly too intense on its own.
3. Meanwhile char the paneer: under the broiler on the foiled sheet close to the element (or in a smoking-hot cast-iron), turning once, until the marinade blackens in spots — 5–8 min. Don't stir it into the gravy raw; the char is the tikka.
4. Cream into the gravy, then the paneer; simmer 3–4 min so the cubes drink some sauce. Finish with a squeeze of lemon, taste salt again, cilantro over the top. Serve on basmati.
