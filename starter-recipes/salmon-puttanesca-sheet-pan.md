## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 4 salmon fillets, ~6 oz each [1 — or the thawed ~1 lb freezer remainder at 3 portions]
- 1½ lb baby gold potatoes, halved [2]
- 1½ lb cherry or grape tomatoes [1]
- ¾ cup pitted Kalamata olives, halved [2]
- 3 tbsp capers, drained [P]
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced [P]
- ¾ tsp red pepper flakes — puttanesca is not shy [P]
- Olive oil, salt [P]
- 1 lemon [P]
- Big handful parsley, chopped [2]
- Optional: 2 anchovy fillets, minced into the tomato toss [2]

## Prep
- Oven to 450°F.
- Halve the potatoes.
- **Tomato mix:** thinly slice the garlic; halve the olives; drain the capers; mince the anchovies if using.
- **Salmon:** pat dry and season with salt.
- Chop the parsley; juice the lemon.

## Steps
1. Toss the potatoes with olive oil and a heavy pinch of salt; roast cut-side down on a sheet pan ~20 min until deeply browned — they're most of the way done before the fish shows up.
2. Meanwhile toss the tomatoes, olives, capers, garlic, pepper flakes (and anchovies if using) with 2 tbsp olive oil.
3. Push the potatoes to one end, tip the tomato mix onto the open pan, and nestle the salmon on top of it. Roast 10–12 min until the salmon hits 125–130°F and the tomatoes blister and slump.
4. Plate the salmon, then crush a third of the tomatoes into the pan juices with the lemon juice — that's the sauce. Spoon it generously over the fish and potatoes, shower with parsley.
