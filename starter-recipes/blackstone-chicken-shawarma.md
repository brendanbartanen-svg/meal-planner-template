## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs [1]
- **Marinade:** ¾ cup Greek yogurt [P] · 3 tbsp lemon juice [P] · 6 cloves garlic, minced [P] · 2 tbsp olive oil [P] · 1 tbsp smoked paprika [P] · 2 tsp cumin [P] · 2 tsp ground coriander [P] · 1 tsp turmeric [P] · ½ tsp cinnamon [P] · ½–1 tsp cayenne [P] · 2 tsp salt · plenty of black pepper
- **Garlic-yogurt sauce:** 1 cup Greek yogurt [P] · 2 cloves garlic, grated [P] · 1 tbsp lemon juice [P] · ½ tsp salt · drizzle of olive oil [P]
- **Quick-pickled onion:** 1 red onion, thinly sliced [2] · ½ cup red wine vinegar [P] · 1 tsp sugar or honey [P] · 1 tsp salt
- 4 pitas [2]
- 2 roma tomatoes, chopped [2]
- 1 cucumber, chopped [2]
- Parsley, roughly chopped [2]

## Prep
- **Chicken (the night before):** mince the garlic, whisk the marinade, and coat the thighs — leave them whole. Overnight in the fridge; 4 h is the floor.
- **Pickled onion:** slice the red onion thin and cover it with the vinegar, sweetener, and salt — 30 min ahead is plenty.
- Blackstone on **high** to heat, well oiled.
- **Garlic-yogurt sauce:** grate the garlic, stir the sauce together, refrigerate.
- **Salad:** chop the tomatoes and cucumber, salt lightly. Rough-chop the parsley.

## Steps
1. Lay the thighs flat on the hot griddle and leave them alone 4–5 min a side — you want deep, charred-in-spots crust; the yogurt marinade browns fast, that's the shawarma flavor.
2. Shawarma move: pull the thighs to the cooler side, chop them roughly with the spatulas right on the griddle, then spread the pile over the hot zone 1–2 min so the cut edges crisp in the rendered fat.
3. Steam-toast the pitas on the griddle, ~30 sec a side.
4. Pile: pita, chicken, garlic-yogurt sauce, pickled onion, salad, parsley. Wrap or eat open-faced.

## Notes
<!-- Cook-to-cook learnings -->
- The chop-and-recrisp on the griddle (step 2) is what separates this from grilled-chicken-in-a-pita — don't skip it.
