## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 1.5 lb large shrimp, peeled/deveined [1]
- **Rub:** 5 cloves garlic, minced [P] · 2 tsp smoked paprika [P] · 1 tsp cumin [P] · **1 tsp red pepper flakes** [P] · ¼ tsp cayenne [P] · 2 tbsp olive oil [P] · salt
- **Chile-garlic butter (the fix — don't skip):** 3 tbsp butter [P] · 3 cloves garlic, minced [P] · 1–2 tbsp chili-garlic sauce or hot sauce [P] · zest + juice of ½ lemon [P]
- 2 zucchini, half-moons [2]
- 2 bell peppers, strips [1]
- 1 red onion, wedges [2]
- 1½ cups jasmine rice [P]
- **Rice seasoning:** 1 tbsp olive oil [P] · ½ tsp cumin [P] · salt · juice of ½ lemon [P]
- Big handful chopped parsley or cilantro [2]

## Prep
- Start the rice.
- Blackstone on medium-high to heat.
- **Shrimp:** pat the thawed shrimp dry. Mince all the garlic, rub and butter both. Toss the shrimp with the full rub and let it sit while the griddle heats.
- **Veg:** zucchini into half-moons, peppers into strips, onion into wedges.
- **Chile-garlic butter:** zest and juice the half lemon; butter, garlic, chili-garlic sauce, zest, and juice into a small pan.
- **Rice seasoning:** juice the other half lemon and chop the herbs; oil and cumin measured alongside.

## Steps
1. Melt the chile-garlic butter ingredients together in the small pan (or a corner of the griddle) — keep it warm and loose.
2. Char the zucchini, peppers, and onion in oil with salt, ~6–8 min, moving pieces to the cool side as they finish.
3. Crank the hot side; shrimp ~90 sec per side until just opaque. Kill the heat.
4. Pour the chile-garlic butter over the shrimp on the griddle and toss hard for 20–30 sec so it coats everything. Scrape the veg back through it too.
5. Fold the rice seasoning (oil, cumin, salt, lemon, herbs) into the rice — don't serve it plain. Pile veg and shrimp on top with all the butter from the griddle.

## Notes
