## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 2 NY strip steaks, ~12 oz each [1]
- Salt, pepper [P]
- 2 tbsp butter [P]
- 2 cloves garlic, smashed [P]
- Fresh thyme or rosemary sprigs [P]
- Neutral oil for searing [P]
- 1.5–2 lb broccoli (crowns or florets) [1]
- Olive oil [P]
- Red pepper flakes, optional [P]

## Prep
- Sous vide bath to 130°F.
- **Steaks:** season with salt and pepper; smash the garlic; bag with the butter, garlic and herb sprigs.
- **Broccoli:** cut into florets if using crowns. Oven to 425°F with ~30 min left on the steaks.

## Steps
1. Steaks into the bath once it's at temp: 130°F for 1–2 hrs (medium-rare).
2. Toss the broccoli with olive oil, salt, pepper (+ red pepper flakes if using); roast 18–20 min until charred at the edges.
3. Pat the steaks dry, sear hard in a screaming-hot pan or on the Blackstone, ~45–60 sec per side, basting with butter.
4. Rest 5 min, slice against the grain, serve over the broccoli.

## Notes
<!-- Cook-to-cook learnings -->
- Veg is flexible — roasted broccoli or roasted potatoes both work; swap freely based on what's on hand.
- Red-meat treat night — plan for **every 3–4 weeks**, not the standard 1-week repeat gap.
