## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs [1]
- **Marinade:** 3 tbsp olive oil [P] · juice + zest of 1 lemon [P] · 4 cloves garlic, minced [P] · 1 tbsp dried oregano [P] · 1 tsp smoked paprika [P] · ½ tsp red pepper flakes [P] · salt, pepper
- 3 bell peppers, thick strips [2/1]
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes [2]
- ½ cup kalamata olives [2]
- 1 red onion, wedges [2]

**Whipped feta:**
- 8 oz feta [1]
- ⅓ cup Greek yogurt [P]
- 1 tbsp olive oil [P]
- Squeeze of lemon [P]
- Black pepper

## Prep
- **Chicken:** zest and juice the lemon, mince the garlic, whisk the marinade, and toss the chicken in it — 30 min, or morning-of.
- Oven to 425°F.
- **Vegetables:** peppers into thick strips, onion into wedges.
- **Whipped feta (now, or while it roasts):** blitz the feta, yogurt, olive oil, lemon, and pepper until smooth and fluffy — a food processor is best, but a fork and some patience work.
- Measure out the olives.

## Steps
1. Spread the peppers, onion, and tomatoes on a sheet pan with oil and salt. Nestle the chicken among them, skinned-side up.
2. Roast 25–30 min until the chicken hits 175°F and the tomatoes have burst and started to caramelize. Scatter the olives over in the last 5 min so they warm without drying.
3. Spread the whipped feta across a platter, pile the chicken and vegetables on top, and pour every bit of pan juice over. Serve as-is, or with pita or rice.

## Notes
<!-- Cook-to-cook learnings -->
- 175°F on thighs, not 165 — thighs are better with the extra few degrees and won't dry out.
- The whipped feta is the point: it's the rich, acidic base that the two 5-rated dishes share. Don't serve the chicken naked on the plate.
