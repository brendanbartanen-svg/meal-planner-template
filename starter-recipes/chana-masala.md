## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 2 cans chickpeas, drained [P — 15-oz cans]
- 1 tbsp ghee or neutral oil [P]
- 1 tsp cumin seeds [P]
- 1 large yellow onion, finely diced [P]
- 4 cloves garlic, minced [P]
- 1 tbsp grated ginger [P]
- 1 serrano or jalapeño, minced (seeds out = medium) [2]
- 2 tsp ground coriander, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp paprika [P]
- ~1¾ cups crushed tomatoes [P — half a 28-oz can]
- 1 tsp garam masala (finish) [P]
- Juice of ½ lemon [P]
- Cilantro [2]
- 1½ cups basmati rice [P]
- Raita: 1 cup Greek yogurt [P], ½ cucumber grated & squeezed [2], pinch ground cumin + salt [P]

## Prep
- Start the basmati.
- **Raita:** grate and squeeze the cucumber; stir it into the yogurt with the cumin and salt; refrigerate.
- **Masala:** finely dice the onion; mince the garlic, grate the ginger, mince the chile.
- **Spices:** ground spices into one small bowl; cumin seeds and ghee by the stove. Measure out the tomatoes. Drain the chickpeas.
- **Finish:** measure the garam masala, juice the lemon half, chop the cilantro.

## Steps
1. Bloom the cumin seeds in the ghee over medium-high until fragrant, ~30 sec.
2. Add the onion with a pinch of salt; cook hard until deeply golden, 8–10 min. Add the garlic, ginger, chile; 1 min.
3. Add the ground spices, toast 30 sec, then the tomatoes. Simmer 5 min until thickened and the oil separates.
4. Add the chickpeas + ½ cup water; simmer 10–12 min, mashing a few against the pot to thicken.
5. Finish with the garam masala and lemon. Serve over rice with raita and cilantro.
