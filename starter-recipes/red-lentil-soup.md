## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 3 tbsp olive oil [P]
- 1 large yellow onion, chopped [P]
- 4 cloves garlic, minced [P]
- 2 tbsp tomato paste [P]
- 1½ tsp ground cumin [P] · ½ tsp chili powder [P] · ¼ tsp cayenne [P]
- ~2 tsp kosher salt total, plus black pepper [P] — the soup reads flat until it's all there
- 1 quart chicken stock or better-than-bouillon equivalent [P] + 3 cups water
- 1½ cups red lentils, rinsed [P]
- 2 carrots, peeled and diced [2 — small bag]
- Juice of 1 lemon, plus wedges from a second [P]
- ½ cup chopped cilantro, leaves and tender stems [2]
- **Chile oil (the finish):** 2 tbsp olive oil [P] · 1 tsp red pepper flakes (Aleppo or Urfa if there is any) [P] · ½ tsp smoked paprika [P] · pinch of ground cumin [P]
- 4 pitas, warmed [2 — bakery; freeze on shop day, see prep_ahead]
- Optional: Greek yogurt, a spoon per bowl [P]

## Prep
- Chop the onion; mince the garlic; peel and dice the carrots; rinse the lentils.
- Measure the cumin, chili powder and cayenne into one small bowl; stock and water measured out.
- Juice the lemon; cut the second into wedges; chop the cilantro.
- **Chile oil:** combine the oil, pepper flakes, smoked paprika and cumin in a small pan, off the heat.
- **Pitas:** oven to 350°F if warming them there (a burner flame works too).

## Steps
1. Heat the olive oil in the Dutch oven over high until shimmering. Onion and garlic in, sauté until the edges go golden, about 4 min — don't rush this; the browned onion is most of the soup's depth.
2. Tomato paste, the spice bowl, ½ tsp salt and ½ tsp black pepper. Stir and fry 2 min until the paste darkens a shade and smells toasted.
3. Add the stock, water, lentils and carrot. Bring to a simmer, partly cover, drop to medium-low. Simmer ~30 min, stirring now and then, until the lentils have collapsed and the carrot is soft. Add another 1 tsp salt partway through.
4. Purée half the pot with the immersion blender (or pull half into the blender and return it) — somewhat chunky, not smooth. Taste: it should be assertively salted, warm from the cumin, with the chile showing at the end. Fix salt now; nothing later will.
5. Chile oil: warm the small pan over medium until it just starts to sizzle, ~1 min, then off the heat so the spices don't scorch.
6. Pitas into the oven or directly on a burner flame for a minute a side. Stir the lemon juice and cilantro into the soup off the heat. Bowls, chile oil spooned over the top, a lemon wedge and a pita each.
