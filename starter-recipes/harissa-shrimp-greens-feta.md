## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 1½ lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined, **fresh case** [1/2 — the warehouse club fresh seafood counter if stocked, else the supermarket seafood counter; never a frozen bag. The ~0.5 lb freezer remainder can cover a third of this — see prep_ahead]
- **Marinade:** 6 tbsp harissa paste [2 — Mina or similar 10-oz jar, a spicy one if they have it] · 6 cloves garlic, grated [P] · 1 tbsp ground cumin [P] · 1 tbsp olive oil [P] · zest + juice of 1 lime [P] · 1 tsp kosher salt [P] · black pepper [P] · 1 tsp red pepper flakes [P] only if the harissa is a mild brand
- 1 lb chard or kale, leaves and stems chopped into 1-inch pieces [2 — 2 bunches; chard is the better choice here for the stems; the warehouse club kale bag works if the remainder has a lunch plan]
- 3 tbsp olive oil, for the greens and the finish [P]
- 2 medium avocados, sliced [2 — 2; or a warehouse club bag with the rest going to lunches and toast]
- ¾ cup chopped red onion [2 — about 1 medium]
- ¾ cup crumbled feta [1 — ~4 oz; the warehouse club block; the remainder keeps for weeks and covers the next skillet turn]
- ¾ cup chopped cilantro [2 — 1 bunch]
- 2 more limes, in wedges [P]
- 1¼ cups basmati or jasmine rice [P] (whole-wheat couscous [2] if you'd rather)
- Kosher salt, black pepper [P]

## Prep
- **Shrimp:** grate the garlic; zest and juice the lime. Salt the shrimp directly with all of the marinade's salt, then toss with the rest of the marinade and a good grind of pepper. Taste a dab — it should read medium-hot; if the harissa is a mild jar, add the pepper flakes now. Cover and refrigerate — 30 min floor, morning-of for real depth.
- **Oven:** rack about 3 inches under the broiler element, oven to 400°F.
- Rice on.
- **Greens:** chop the chard or kale, leaves and stems, into 1-inch pieces.
- **Toppings:** chop the red onion and cilantro; crumble the feta; cut the limes into wedges. Slice the avocados last and squeeze a wedge over them so they hold.

## Steps
1. Greens on a half-sheet pan with 2 tbsp of the oil, a real pinch of salt, and pepper; toss by hand, spread even, and pour ¾ cup water into the pan — the water steams the stems while the leaves roast.
2. Top rack, 10–12 min, stirring once with tongs, until the leaves are wilted and the stems have softened with a little crunch left. Push the greens to one side of the pan and switch the oven to broil.
3. Shrimp in a single layer on the empty side, every bit of marinade scraped over them, the rest of the oil drizzled over shrimp and greens. Broil 6–9 min, rotating once, until the shrimp are just opaque and browned at the edges and the greens are charred in spots. Pull early rather than late — a full pan of large shrimp under a strong broiler is closer to 6 than 9.
4. Toss the shrimp and greens together on the pan so the greens pick up the harissa juices, and taste the juices for salt and lime. Over rice: avocado, red onion, feta, and cilantro piled on top, lime wedges for squeezing. The feta and avocado against the harissa are the finishing element — don't skimp.

## Notes
