## Ingredients
<!-- [1] primary store, [2] secondary store, [P] pantry -->
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs [1]
- **Marinade:** 1 cup Greek yogurt [P] · 2 tbsp lemon juice [P] · 1 tbsp grated ginger [P] · 5 cloves garlic, minced [P] · 2 tsp garam masala [P] · 2 tsp ground coriander [P] · 1 tsp cumin [P] · 1 tsp turmeric [P] · 1 tsp smoked paprika [P] · ½–1 tsp cayenne [P] · 2 tsp salt
- 2 zucchini, thick planks [2]
- 2 bell peppers, halved [2/1]
- 1 red onion, thick rounds [2]
- Olive oil, salt [P]
- **Raita:** 1 cup Greek yogurt [P] · ½ cucumber, grated and squeezed dry [2] · ¼ tsp ground cumin [P] · salt · mint or cilantro [2]
- 1½ cups basmati rice [P] or naan
- 1 lemon, halved [P]

## Prep
- **Marinade (the night before, 4 h floor):** grate the ginger, mince the garlic, whisk with the rest of the marinade, and coat the thighs. This is a yogurt marinade doing real work on the texture, not a quick toss.
- Fire the kamado to **~450–500°F direct**. The high radiant heat is the whole point here: it's the closest thing you have to a tandoor, and it chars the marinade before the thighs dry out.
- Rice on (unless it's naan).
- **Raita:** grate the cucumber and squeeze it dry; chop the mint or cilantro; stir together with the yogurt, cumin and salt; refrigerate.
- **Vegetables:** cut the zucchini into thick planks, halve and seed the peppers, cut the onion into thick rounds; oil and salt them.
- Halve the lemon.

## Steps
1. Grill the vegetables first, 3–4 min a side, and pull them to a platter.
2. Grill the chicken, scraping off only the drips — you want a thick coat of marinade going on. About 4–5 min a side until charred in patches and 175°F internal. Put the lemon halves cut-side down on the grate for the last 2 min.
3. Rest 5 min, slice, squeeze the charred lemon over everything. Serve with rice or naan, the vegetables, and a generous spoonful of raita.

## Notes
<!-- Cook-to-cook learnings -->
- 175°F on thighs, not 165 — they're better with the extra few degrees and won't dry out.
- Don't wipe the marinade off. The charred yogurt crust is the dish.
